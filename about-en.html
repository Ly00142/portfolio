About me

// After two years of pre-engineering studies at Sup'Management, I graduated from ESAIP as an engineer in risk management and environment, majoring in industrial and natural risks. I specialise in occupational health and safety.

My internships took me through agri-food, industrial electronics and cosmetics: hazardous energy control at ADM, environmental analysis at Crouzet, quality and safety of weighing areas at L'Oréal. Two Erasmus semesters, in Liberec and then Toledo, taught me to work in English and Spanish.

Since September 2026 I have been on a work-study contract at Danone, in the Global Health, Safety and Wellbeing team. I support the rollout of group safety initiatives, coordinate safety e-learning for sites worldwide and consolidate safety KPIs in Sphera.
